# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful, assume good faith, review the submission and not the person, and keep
discussions about the resources themselves.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer through the contact listed on the repository owner's
profile. Reports are handled confidentially, and the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant
apply.

Promoting a product through repeated or undisclosed submissions is not a conduct violation on its own,
but such pull requests are closed under the rules in CONTRIBUTING.md.
